BPTracking — Privacy Policy
BigProject · Android app BPTracking (com.bigproject.BPTracking)
Effective date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BigProject ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the BPTracking mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Data controller: BigProject
App: BPTracking (vehicle GPS tracking and alarms)
Contact: contact@caralarms.gr

2. Information we collect

Account
  What: Email address and password (for login)
  How: You provide these when you register or sign in (Supabase Auth).

Profile
  What: Display name you choose
  How: Stored when you save your profile in the App.

Linked devices
  What: Tracker ID, vehicle name, brand, model, year, license plate (optional)
  How: You add or edit vehicles in the App.

Location / GPS
  What: Latitude, longitude, speed, timestamps, and related telemetry from your GPS trackers
  How: Sent by your hardware trackers to our servers; shown in maps and trip history. We do not collect phone GPS unless you grant location permission for map features on your device.

Push notifications
  What: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token, platform (Android)
  How: Generated when you allow notifications, to send vehicle alarm alerts.

Technical
  What: App version, device type, basic logs needed to operate the service
  How: Automatically when you use the App and our backend (Supabase, Firebase).

3. How we use your information

- Authenticate you and manage your account
- Display live and historical vehicle positions on maps
- Link tracker hardware to your user account
- Send push notifications when a vehicle alarm condition is detected
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability of the service
- Respond to support requests and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

- Contract: to provide the tracking service you signed up for
- Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, service improvement
- Consent: where required (e.g. push notifications on your device)
- Legal obligation: when law requires retention or disclosure

5. Third-party services

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf:

- Supabase — authentication, database (profiles, devices, GPS records, push tokens)
- Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications
- Google Maps — map display (subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy)

These providers may process data in the EU, US, or other regions. We use contractual and technical safeguards appropriate to the service.

6. Data sharing

We may share information only:

- With service providers listed above, under confidentiality obligations
- When required by law, court order, or government request
- To protect rights, safety, or security of users or the public
- With your explicit consent

7. Data retention

- Account and profile data: kept while your account is active; deleted after a verified deletion request (within 30 days).
- Linked devices and push tokens: deleted with your account or when you remove them in the App.
- GPS / tracker telemetry: stored by tracker hardware ID and retained for operational purposes; may remain after account deletion unless you request removal of specific tracker history.
- Backups: may retain deleted data up to 90 days before automatic purge.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent (e.g. disable notifications in device settings)
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email contact@caralarms.gr.
To delete your account, use the account deletion page published with this policy
(same GitHub Pages site as this file: delete-account.html).

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and row-level security on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

BPTracking is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

If you use the App from outside the country where our servers are hosted, your information may be transferred internationally. We take steps to ensure an adequate level of protection where required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version at the same URL and update the effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: contact@caralarms.gr
